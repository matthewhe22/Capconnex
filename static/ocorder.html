How it works

Four stages. Zero spreadsheets.

Request raised

Owners or the committee log a request via the portal or email — automatically captured, tagged, and assigned.

Quotes collected

Send to multiple vendors, compare side-by-side, capture committee approvals with full audit trail.

Job executed

Vendor receives the job, uploads photos and notes on completion, owners see live progress.

Invoice & archive

Invoice attached to the job, payment status tracked, full history preserved for AGM reporting and audits.
