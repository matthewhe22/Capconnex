About Capconnex

A decade at the intersection of property, capital, and code.

Founded in Melbourne in 2014, Capconnex sits where real estate investment, property valuation, and software engineering meet — and builds the tools that should exist there.

Our story

We started in the work, not the slides.

Capconnex began as a Melbourne-based investment practice working across residential and mixed-use property — running feasibilities, advising on capital structure, and supporting valuations for institutional and private clients.

Over the years, the same friction kept reappearing in our own work: brittle spreadsheets, slow reporting cycles, energy systems that wasted money no one could see. So we started writing software for ourselves. The tools worked — and the people we worked with started asking to use them too.

Today, Capconnex operates as an investment-and-applied-AI studio. We still do the underlying property and energy work — and we ship the platforms that come out of it.

Milestones
  • 2014 Capconnex founded in Melbourne. Initial focus: feasibility and valuation work for residential and mixed-use developments.
  • 2018–2022 Internal feasibility models grow into a structured platform; first energy-management experiments on our own infrastructure.
  • 2024 Pencil Feasibility Model formalised as a product. AI-assisted benchmarking and version control added.
  • 2025 Smart EV Charging Manager goes live — orchestrating solar, battery and Tesla charging end-to-end.
  • 2026 Three production platforms — Pencil, Smart EV Charging, and OC Order — used across our own portfolio and partner organisations.

How we operate

Five principles that keep us honest.

01

Built by operators

Everything we ship has to solve a problem we've felt in our own work first.

02

Transparent by default

No black boxes. Every assumption, every formula is visible — you need to be able to defend the output.

03

We'd rather be exceptional for property and energy professionals than mediocre for everyone.
