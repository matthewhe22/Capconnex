How it works

Smart charging in four steps.

From sunrise to spirited drives — intelligent orchestration of your Alpha ESS battery, solar, and Tesla Wall Connector.

Solar + battery monitor

Alpha ESS reads solar generation, battery state-of-charge, and household load in real time — sampled every 2 minutes.

Formula engine

Available power = Solar + Battery − Household Load. If surplus drops below 200 W, charging pauses cleanly.

Tesla Wall Connector

Charge current is adjusted dynamically via the Tesla API. No app needed; no manual override required.

Recalculate & repeat

Every 2 minutes, on the clock. Charges from 5 A up to 32 A. Zero grid import. Zero touch.
